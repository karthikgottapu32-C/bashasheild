# BhashaShield Known Limitations & Residual Risk Assessment

## 1. Scope & Adversarial Realism

1. **Synthetic Training & Evaluation**:
   Current quantitative metrics (e.g. 96.0% precision, 100% recall) are evaluated on synthetic benchmarks with strict template family separation. In real-world enterprise deployments, human adversaries may employ novel slang, localized dialect variations, and complex semantic metaphors not captured in synthetic templates.

2. **Long-Context Indirect Injection**:
   While BhashaShield bounds inputs at 10,000 characters by default, very long documents (50,000+ tokens) in RAG pipelines require pre-chunking. An indirect prompt injection embedded deep within an innocuous 100-page PDF may dilute n-gram statistical density if not chunked prior to inspection.

3. **Multi-Turn Semantic Drift**:
   Stateful multi-turn conversational drift where an attacker splits a jailbreak across 10 seemingly benign interaction turns cannot be caught solely on a single stateless prompt call. Operators must submit cumulative conversation buffers to `context` in `/v1/decision`.

---

## 2. Supported Language Boundaries

- **Fully Supported & Validated**: Telugu, Hindi, Romanized Telugu, Romanized Hindi, English, and Mixed combinations.
- **Experimental / Rule-Assisted**: Tamil, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, Urdu. While Unicode normalization and homoglyph defense apply universally to all scripts, specific grammatical override patterns for non-Telugu/Hindi Indic languages require further template expansion.

---

## 3. Recommended Compensating Controls

- **Output Filtering**: Always pair input guardrails (BhashaShield) with output guardrails to prevent accidental leakage of sensitive tokens generated by the LLM.
- **Model Privilege Sandboxing**: Never grant AI assistants unconditional tool access (e.g. arbitrary SQL or terminal execution) without human-in-the-loop confirmation.
- **Continuous Fine-Tuning**: Regularly harvest anonymized, flagged queries from `/scans` to expand the retraining loop in `backend/app/ml/train.py`.
